Important interpretation notes

Price gain is not guaranteed profit. The tables compare currently supplied odds, not executable prices, available liquidity, or matched betting results. Commissions and market rules vary, and your existing price-consistency safeguards can reject genuine outliers as well as incorrect labels.

Verified betfair_ex_uk is the only baseline when provider keys are present. Generic “Betfair” quotes and unsupported markets are excluded. Favourite/underdog classification requires verified Betfair back prices for both opponents; otherwise it is shown as Unknown. Golf futures and other outright markets are not assigned a favourite based on partial listings. The bookmaker filter recalculates the best competitor among selected providers. Quote age uses provider timestamps when present, never the local download time.
